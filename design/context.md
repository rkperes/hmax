### Cores

The palette is structured in two roles:

- **Base** — #000000 (black): dominant background, sets a premium/technical tone
- **Hydro** — #3AA8C1 (teal): primary accent, used for the logo symbol, icons, highlights, and interactive elements
- **Max** — #C4CBCF (light grey): secondary surface/text, used on dark backgrounds for wordmark and supporting UI

The naming "Hydro / Max" directly ties the palette split to the brand name, reinforcing the two-part identity across all applications.

### Aplicações da Identidade Visual

The brand defines three core values, each expressed through a distinct geometric form derived from the logo's visual language. These are intended as a design system for web pages — each value can anchor a section, hero, or feature block with its corresponding shape as a graphic element.

1. **Robustez** (Robustness) — represented by a solid, slightly perspective-skewed rectangular block. Use for sections about product quality, materials, or durability. The form is heavy, grounded, and stable.

2. **Versatilidade** (Versatility) — represented by a single diagonal slash/parallelogram. Use for sections about product range, adaptability, or applications across sectors. The form is dynamic and directional.

3. **Amplitude** (Reach/Scale) — represented by the logo symbol itself (the interlocking angular brackets). Use for sections about the company's scope, global reach, or breadth of solutions. The form combines both other elements and closes the visual system.

All three shapes use the teal (#3AA8C1) on light/neutral grey backgrounds, consistent with the brand's light-mode application.

---

### Logo

The logo is composed of two elements: a geometric symbol and a wordmark.

The symbol is an abstract interlocking shape made of two angular, bracket-like pieces in teal (#3AA8C1) that form a square negative space at the center — evoking structure, precision, and mechanical assembly, consistent with heavy equipment / crane industry context.

The wordmark "HYDROMAX" uses a custom industrial typeface with sharp, angular cuts, reinforcing a technical and bold character. On dark backgrounds the wordmark renders in light grey (#C4CBCF); on light backgrounds it renders in black (#000000).

The branding intent is: authoritative and technical, but modern — avoiding the dated aesthetics common in the crane/heavy machinery sector.
